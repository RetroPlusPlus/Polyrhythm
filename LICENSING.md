## 2. Commercial license

Any commercial use requires a separate **commercial license**. Commercial use includes, without
limitation: shipping or selling a product built with the engine, using it in a business or for
commercial advantage, and offering the engine — or a service based on it — to others. The
commercial license grants the rights the noncommercial license withholds.

To obtain a commercial license, contact the maintainer.
